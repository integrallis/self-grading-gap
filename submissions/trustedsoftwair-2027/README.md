## Confirmed for 2027

The [official researchR call](https://conf.researchr.org/home/icse-2027/trustedsoftwair-2027) offers early-stage research ideas with an **eight-page limit**, plus abstracts of already-published papers for non-archival presentation. Its scope includes testing, trustworthy synthesis, and evaluation. It links a working [2027 HotCRP portal](https://icse2027-trustedsoftwair.hotcrp.com/); the public page currently says submissions are closed.

The [central ICSE workshop schedule](https://conf.researchr.org/track/icse-2027/icse-2027-workshops) gives submission **13 November 2026 AoE (UTC−12)**, notification **11 December 2026**, and camera-ready **29 January 2027**. It schedules this workshop on **26 April 2027 in Dublin**. Central policy prohibits refereed submissions by workshop organizers and their currently directly supervised students.

## Unresolved instructions

The linked [external call](https://trustedsoftwair.github.io/cfp) says the call is not yet open and leaves formatting and dates unspecified. It describes all accepted contributions as non-archival and permits already-published or concurrently submitted papers when identified. The researchR call instead distinguishes early research from abstracts of published papers. Those pages do not establish one complete, consistent contribution/eligibility policy.

Confirm template, anonymity, references and appendices within the eight pages, and whether the external non-archival/concurrent-submission policy applies to the research-paper route. Do not infer published-paper eligibility from an arXiv submission being on hold. The published-paper abstract route requires confirming that the submitted work qualifies under the final call.

## Preparation decisions

Retain the distinction between passing tests and proving correctness. Frame the contribution around evaluation, independent evidence, and the limits of generated acceptance criteria. Use eight pages as the planning ceiling; do not manufacture a proof contribution or select a final template from the parent conference's research track. The guide's one-active-review rule remains the team's plan despite the external page's broader permission.
