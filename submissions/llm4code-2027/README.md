## Confirmed for 2027

- The [official ICSE listing](https://conf.researchr.org/home/icse-2027/llm4code-2027) links to the workshop's external website.
- The [central ICSE workshop schedule](https://conf.researchr.org/track/icse-2027/icse-2027-workshops) sets submission **13 November 2026 AoE (UTC−12)**, notification **11 December 2026**, and camera-ready **29 January 2027**. It lists the workshop on **1 May 2027 in Dublin**. The workshop's [dates page](https://llm4code.github.io/dates/) agrees on the deadlines but labels them tentative.
- Central policy caps regular workshop papers at **eight pages**, and short papers at five. It does not specify whether references are excluded. It also prohibits refereed submissions by workshop organizers and their currently directly supervised students.

These details must be reconfirmed for 2027; the ACM class is not established as this variant's final template.
