# Statistical and evidence review

Review date: 2026-09-13. This review concerns the manuscript and existing recorded
experiments, not new model experiments. Numerical corrections and sensitivity analyses
are generated by [the post hoc audit runner](../experiments/review_audit/run_audit.py)
into [its analysis](../experiments/review_audit/results/ANALYSIS.md) and
[machine-readable output](../experiments/review_audit/results/audit.json). Original raw
records remain untouched. Manuscript subsection names below identify the reviewed claims;
line numbers refer to instrument/registration sources and are stable through prose edits.

## Release-blocking scientific corrections

1. **HumanEval mixes two oracles in one purported confusion matrix.**
   `experiments/exp006_model_asymmetry/run_asym.py:208` counts base-oracle passes;
   lines 211–215 count plus-oracle false accepts and base-oracle false rejects.
   `experiments/exp011_verifier_generalization/run_he.py:239` repeats this convention.
   The existing values are faithful to those runners but were mislabeled as one strong
   oracle. The complete HumanEval+ pass/FA/FR means are control 22.6/4.0/5.0,
   strong-test 24.2/3.2/3.6, strong-judge 23.4/4.4/2.2, both 24.8/3.4/1.6.
   **Correction:** report separate complete base and plus matrices, or use the complete
   plus matrix consistently and label this as a post hoc reporting correction. Update
   all pass/FR deltas, plots, captions, and narrative statements. Do not describe the
   original mixed FA-plus + FR-base composite as one oracle's total error.

2. **Non-rejection is repeatedly described as equivalence or impossibility.**
   The experiment decision rule tests whether an improvement is large and statistically
   detectable; it cannot establish “invariance,” “flat,” “unchanged,” or that stronger
   judges cannot help. LCB FA point estimates fall substantially in several cells. The
   package-cluster bootstrap gives a strong-minus-control RGRBench conditional-FA
   difference of +0.5pp with interval [-19.4, 20.8] at the shared threshold and +6.0pp
   [-9.4, 21.5] at the adaptive 0.50 threshold. These admit substantial improvement and
   harm. **Correction:** use “none of the original FA contrasts met the registered
   decision rule,” describe directions and uncertainty, and remove claims of a
   fundamental impossibility or universal failure of verifier strength.

3. **Benchmark failure is not necessarily violation of the supplied specification.**
   The `numbers_to_words` appendix explicitly says the specification leaves exception
   type unspecified while the oracle requires `ValueError`. Such an implementation can
   satisfy the supplied text and still fail the oracle. This contradicts the definition
   paragraph's inference that every gap member is necessarily specification
   misinterpretation, and the “fully specified requirement” caption.
   **Correction:** define the measured outcome as oracle-relative disagreement, identify
   this example as a specification/oracle contract mismatch, and state that a blinded
   adjudication of all failures is needed before interpreting the full rate as model
   semantic errors. Mutation testing does not resolve this mismatch.

4. **A mutation score and zero observed adapter failures are not semantic error bounds.**
   Mutation kill rate measures detection on chosen syntactic perturbations, not oracle
   false-positive rate or coverage over all specification behaviors. Adapter calibration
   selects fixtures and excludes failed behavior-preservation checks before testing
   adapters (`calibrate_adapter.py:145–160`). It observes no failures in 13 retained
   packages with 5 exclusions. Its recorded `adapter_error` is a test-failure fraction
   (`calibrate_adapter.py:184–187`), not the binary package failure endpoint defining the
   gap. Even a hypothetical IID binomial model gives a one-sided upper bound of 20.6%,
   not zero; fixture selection makes this merely illustrative.
   **Correction:** say “mutation-characterized suites” and “no adapter failures observed
   on the selected calibration cases.” Remove “error bound 0.000,” claims that adapter
   effects are excluded, and assertions of measured semantic oracle accuracy.

## Statistical protocol and interpretation

5. **The original runners omit registered tests and the control-spread adjustment.**
   `run_asym.py:267–278` and `run_lcb.py:271–282` compute p-values only for FA and pass,
   labeling FR and total error by effect size alone. The pre-registrations require the
   maximum observed control spread when larger than the floor, but runners always use
   constant floors. Observed control spans include base-HumanEval FR 5 versus floor 2,
   plus-FA 3 versus floor 2, and LCB FR 4 versus floor 2 and total error 5 versus floor 3.
   **Correction:** disclose the deviations; remove “all floors conservative” and “only
   significant effects” statements. Preserve original findings as historical, and use
   completed tests only as explicit post hoc analyses. The supplement finds nominal
   total-error p=.03125 for HE+ strong-both and LCB strong-judge. It does not establish
   a causal decomposition into gatekeeping and feedback.

6. **The test and reported effect target different quantities.**
   McNemar is applied to each problem's majority-of-five status, whereas effect-size
   thresholds use mean per-run counts. Many changes in frequency disappear on
   dichotomization. Five repeats do not make these tests sensitive to small numbers of
   discordant problem majorities. **Correction:** identify both estimands and limited
   power. Use the post hoc problem-cluster intervals on mean differences as a
   sensitivity analysis, without relabeling them as pre-registered confirmations.
   No multiplicity adjustment is present; original p-values are nominal/unadjusted.

7. **RGRBench Wilson intervals ignore repeated packages.**
   `run_flagship.py:250` and its analysis treat package-run claims as independent
   Bernoulli trials, although each package is repeated three times. The post hoc
   package bootstrap retains all repeated runs and paired package identity across
   cells. The control interval widens from nominal Wilson [30.4, 53.7]% to
   [26.0, 58.2]%; the shared-threshold strong-cell interval is [23.1, 63.4]%.
   **Correction:** label Wilson intervals as nominal run-level summaries; use the
   cluster analysis to discuss contrast uncertainty. Do not claim larger scale is
   established merely because an interval bound exceeds another benchmark's point
   estimate: task selection, prompts, adapters, and oracle contracts differ too.

8. **Conditional claim rates retain selection effects.**
   Matching packages with valid oracle verdicts does not match accepted packages or
   candidate implementations. Matching throughput does not do so either. Invalid
   evaluations also differ by cell. The supplement reports all-missing-oracle bounds:
   shared-threshold strong FA lies in [40.4, 44.2]% when its two excluded claims are
   counted as all correct versus all wrong; control has no excluded claims.
   **Correction:** compare these as pipeline-policy operating points, report absolute
   correct-output yield alongside conditional FA, and retain selection as a limitation.

## Threshold sweep and mechanism

9. **The original exp013 analysis was not reproducible from its supplied analyzer.**
   The analyzer was copied from exp010, pooled all thresholds, and did not load the
   exp010 anchors. It also contained an impossible claim: CI [51.5, 80.4]% was said to
   be entirely above 54%. The analyzer has now been repaired and its table regenerated
   without changing raw data or table values. The original point-estimate H1 band
   criterion is missed at 0.40, but its Wilson interval overlaps the upper band edge.

10. **The threshold intervention and registration claims need correction.**
    `exp013/PRE_REGISTRATION.md:21` and the runner both change RED **and GREEN** thresholds.
    Calling it a RED-only sweep is incorrect. The original grid and H3 selection rule
    enumerate 0.40/0.55/0.70/0.85, not 0.50 (registration lines 26, 48). The 0.50
    follow-up is adaptive and its throughput is not equal to control. The registered
    raw-count selection rule also compares differing run counts.
    **Correction:** identify the adaptive follow-up, compare normalized acceptance
    percentages, avoid claiming independent confirmatory matching or identification of
    a whole tradeoff curve. Point estimates are not monotone at every adjacent setting,
    and the strictest threshold's estimate is below 42%.

11. **Retry, complexity, and feedback effects are observational.**
    Successful outputs requiring retries are a selected harder subset; there is no
    random retry-budget intervention or first-versus-final oracle measurement. Difficulty
    tiers are labeled categories with few accepted hard tasks, not randomized complexity.
    Strong-judge role assignment changes gating, feedback, failure analysis, and eventual
    candidates simultaneously. Strong test authors can also retry RED, so “writes the
    suite only once” is wrong.
    **Correction:** describe associations and a mechanism consistent with telemetry;
    reserve causal attribution for feedback-disabled, verdict-only, fixed-candidate,
    or randomized-retry ablations. Give numerator/denominator for sparse tier subsets.

12. **The verifier-generalization completeness statement has a direct exception.**
    `exp011/results/lcb_gemini/ANALYSIS.md` reports strong-judge FR 3.4 versus control 2.6.
    Gemini's strong-both cell improves, but its judge-only cell does not. **Correction:**
    scope zero-FR and recovered-pass statements to the observed model/role/benchmark
    cells, and report this replication exception.

13. **Efficiency and contamination claims exceed the design.**
    Recorded overall spend does not establish that weak-generator/frontier-judge is an
    efficient configuration without per-cell cost and an explicit alternative on the
    cost/correctness frontier. A post-cutoff problem window reduces exposure risk; it
    does not establish that task ideas are unseen, that strong verifiers are unexposed,
    or that contamination can only improve soundness. **Correction:** remove unsupported
    efficiency rankings and absolute contamination claims, retaining the narrower design
    rationale and evidence.

## What the existing evidence can support

The repository contains repeated examples where executed self-tests accept implementations
that fail separate benchmark tests. This occurs across the selected tasks and verifier
assignments. The original FA contrasts do not meet the registered joint decision rule,
while selected frontier-judge settings improve output or reduce internal false rejects.
Application-scale policies exhibit a substantial oracle-relative claim-error rate and a
large change in output selection. These findings remain useful after replacing
equivalence, impossibility, semantic-oracle, and causal-mechanism claims with the narrower
statements warranted by the recorded data.
