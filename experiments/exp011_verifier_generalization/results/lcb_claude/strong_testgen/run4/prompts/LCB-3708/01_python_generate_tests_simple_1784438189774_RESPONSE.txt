Looking at this specification, I need to understand the zigzag traversal with skipping:

1. Traverse in zigzag: row 0 left-to-right, row 1 right-to-left, row 2 left-to-right, etc.
2. Skip every alternate cell in this traversal order
3. Start at (0,0) and include it (it's the 1st cell, not skipped)

**Example 1:** `[[1,2],[3,4]]`
- Zigzag order: 1 (0,0) → 2 (0,1) → 4 (1,1) → 3 (1,0)
