## Root cause

The implementation fundamentally misunderstands the problem. It simply sums k of the largest existing gaps, but this is not how rescheduling works. Rescheduling k meetings means you can move k meetings to different times, which allows combining multiple gaps into one larger gap. The algorithm should be calculating the maximum contiguous free time achievable by rescheduling at most k meetings (e.g., by packing meetings together), not just summing the k largest gaps.
