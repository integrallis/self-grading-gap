## Root cause

The implementation has an off-by-one error in the online status check. Users come back online when `timestamp > offline_until[user_id]`, but they should come back online when `timestamp >= offline_until[user_id]` (i.e., at exactly the 60-second mark). The condition `timestamp > offline_until` means users stay offline until timestamp is strictly greater than the end of their offline period, making them offline for 61 seconds instead of 60.
