## Root cause

The implementation misunderstands the problem. It assumes that rescheduling k meetings simply removes their duration from the total occupied time. However, the problem requires finding the maximum contiguous free time interval after optimally rescheduling k meetings. The algorithm needs to identify which meetings to reschedule to create the largest gap, not just subtract meeting durations from total time.
