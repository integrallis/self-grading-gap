## Root cause

The implementation uses a sliding window algorithm that finds the maximum sum of a **contiguous subarray** with unique elements. The actual problem requires finding the maximum sum by:
1. Deleting duplicate occurrences (keeping at most one of each value)
2. Selecting a subset (not necessarily contiguous) of the remaining unique elements

These are two fundamentally different problems.
