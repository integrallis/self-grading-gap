### test_large_values_modulo
Claims: `minMaxSums([10**9] * 70, 70) == 140000000`

Computation: With all elements equal to 10^9, every subsequence has min = max = 10^9.
- Number of non-empty subsequences with at most 70 elements from 70 identical elements:
  For r elements (1 ≤ r ≤ 70), there's exactly C(70, r) = 1 way to choose them (all are identical).
  Actually, all subsequences are the same: [10^9, 10^9, ..., 10^9] with varying lengths.
  Total subsequences = Σ(r=1 to 70) C(70, r) = 2^70 - 1
- Each contributes 2 * 10^9
- Total = (2^70 - 1) * 2 * 10^9 mod (10^9 + 7)

2^70 = 1,180,591,620,717,411,303,424
(2^70 - 1) * 2 * 10^9 is astronomically large and needs modular arithmetic.

The claimed answer of 140,000,000 appears to be 70 * 2 * 10^9 mod (10^9 + 7), which would only be correct if there were exactly 70 subsequences, but there are 2^70 - 1 subsequences. **This is incorrect.**
