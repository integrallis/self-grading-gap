**Root cause:** The implementation incorrectly uses `nums[i]` (the **value** at index i) to compute the subarray start position. Looking at the pattern in the test cases:
- The subarray at index i always ends at position i (inclusive)

- Index 0: length 1 subarray
- Index 1: length 2 subarray  
- Index 2+: length 3 subarray
