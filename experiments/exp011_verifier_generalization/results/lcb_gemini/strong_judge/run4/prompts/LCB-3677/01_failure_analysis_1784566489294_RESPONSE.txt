Specifically, when transitioning to a cell `(i, j)` with a negative `coins[i][j]` value, the implementation only considers the outcome where a neutralization is used. It completely fails to calculate the score for the alternative choice: taking the loss.

This means any path that requires taking a loss at a robber cell is either ignored or incorrectly calculated, leading the algorithm to choose a suboptimal path.
